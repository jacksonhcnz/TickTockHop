Tick Tock Hop

Swing the rotating hand from dot to dot and reach the green goal. Avoid enemy hands and mines, and land on blue point dots to score.

Levels 1 to 20, timed. Regular: if you die, retry the level and the clock keeps running. Hard: if you die, back to level 1 and the clock resets.

Or practise a single level (21 to 28 introduce the new objects):

Keyboard: hold A to bounce along · hold S to swing · Space to reverse spin
Controller: hold LB to bounce along · hold RB to swing · X to reverse spin · Start menu
